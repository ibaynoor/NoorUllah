Islamabad, The Beautiful City

Islamabad is the federal capital of Pakistan,
nestled against the picturesque backdrop of the Margalla Hills. Unlike many other South Asian cities,
Islamabad is a meticulously planned city divided into organized sectors, known for its wide, tree-lined avenues, abundant green spaces, and high standard of living.
It serves as the political heart of the country—housing the Parliament, federal government offices, and international embassies—while also being famous for iconic landmarks like the majestic Faisal Mosque.
